The follow-up that never forgets

Most window cleaning jobs aren't lost on price. They're lost because the lead asked at 8pm, nobody replied until morning, and they booked the other company. Here's the exact sequence Rilo would run:

Under 10 sec
Instant text

“Hi Jessica, thanks for reaching out to Rilo Window Cleaning…” — sent before they've even left your website.

1 hour
Gentle nudge (if no reply)

“Just floating this back up — want me to get you a quick quote for the house?”

Next day
Day 2 follow-up

Different angle, helpful not pushy. Offers two time slots for a quote visit.

Day 3
Last check-in

“Want me to close this out, or keep a spot for you this week?” — this one pulls a lot of replies.

Day before
Booking reminder

Once booked, they get a reminder so the quote visit doesn't turn into a no-show.

Questions Rilo might ask

Short answers, in plain words. Tap any question to open it.

“I already text my leads back. Why do I need this?”

When you're free, you do. This covers the times you're on a ladder, driving, or it's 9pm — which is exactly when a lot of homeowners send quote requests. It doesn't replace you, it covers the gaps.

“Will it sound like a robot to my customers?”

No. The messages are short, written in Rilo's voice, and approved by you before anything goes live. And the moment a customer wants a real conversation, you're notified and can take over.

“What do I have to set up?”

Almost nothing. We connect it to your website form and your number, you approve the message wording, and it goes live. You just watch the pipeline fill.

“What if a lead replies with a real question?”

The system handles the simple back-and-forth to get the quote booked, and pings you for anything it shouldn't answer. You never lose control of a conversation.

“Can I see all my leads in one place?”

Yes — that's the pipeline you just saw. New, contacted, in conversation, booked. No digging through texts and emails.
